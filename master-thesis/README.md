# Forecast Atlas

**[Open Forecast Atlas](https://nad-web.github.io/Hands-on-Projects/master-thesis/) · [Simulation](https://nad-web.github.io/Hands-on-Projects/master-thesis/simulation/) · [Documentation](https://nad-web.github.io/Hands-on-Projects/master-thesis/documentation/)**

Forecast Atlas is the public companion to Noorullah Adel’s master’s thesis, *Towards Trustworthy Financial Time Series Forecasting*. Compare forecasts from seven models with what happened in four stock indices, then explore how the study explains those predictions. A separate experiment uses sunspot data.

## Explore the study

- **Forecast Lab** — Pick a date to compare the seven recorded forecasts. Move the lag sliders to find similar dates, or try new inputs in the Linear Regression what-if lab.
- **Research Questions** — Select one of the three questions to see its conclusion and explore the evidence behind it.
- **Learn** — Work through the models, equations, terminology, and visual examples at your own pace.
- **Documentation** — Read all eight executed notebooks with their code, figures, tables, and stored outputs. The methods guide and bibliography sit alongside them.

The simulation uses the submitted research data. The complete thesis manuscript stays private.

## Run on your computer

With Python 3 installed, open `START_HERE.command` on macOS or `START_HERE.bat` on Windows. You can also run `python3 tools/serve.py` from this folder and open the address it prints.

To verify the published data, run `python3 tools/verify.py`. See the [application license](LICENSE-APP) for the app code. The data, notebooks, and figures retain their original terms.
