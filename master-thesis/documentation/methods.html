METHOD / 01

Research scope

The companion examines predictive accuracy, interpretability, temporal explanation stability, diagnostic-specific faithfulness, and economic interpretation separately. It does not combine these into a universal trust score.

The supplied experiment contains Linear Regression, ARIMA, Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, LSTM, and a simplified TFT adapter. The latter is not the full production TFT architecture.

METHOD / 02

Frozen inputs and target definitions

The four financial tasks use adjusted closing levels of the S&P 500, DAX, NASDAQ Composite, and Dow Jones. Daily log returns form the lagged inputs. Forecast targets are cumulative future log returns at horizons one and five trading sessions.

The Monash Sunspot robustness task retains native future values. The supplied 11,109-row execution input is verified against its original TSF. Sunspot is excluded from pooled financial summaries.

METHOD / 03

Chronological fitting and temporal separation

Features are lag_1, lag_5, lag_21, and lag_252. Preprocessing scales are fitted on the exact training interval. Five purge rows and five embargo rows separate training from a 252-row test block.

Rolling training retains the latest 500 training rows; expanding training keeps its start fixed. The implementations use chronological validation tails for the boosted and neural fits, with the documented early-stopping rules.

METHOD / 04

Native and matched coverage

Each static pipeline exports 1,512 native forecasts per setting. Each neural pipeline exports 924, with a 21-row sequence warm-up excluded from each native test block. Native coverage therefore cannot directly serve as the seven-model ranking sample.

The matched synthesis keeps 924 identical origins per model and setting: four blocks of 231. Across five datasets, two horizons, and two schemes this gives 18,480 shared origins and 129,360 model–origin predictions. Matching coverage does not equalize all information sets or tuning choices.

METHOD / 05

Predictive evaluation

RMSE and MAE quantify prediction-error magnitude. Directional accuracy compares financial target signs. R² uses the realized evaluation-target mean as its denominator reference. Persistence means zero future cumulative return for finance and the current native value for Sunspot.

Primary financial summaries weight the 16 series–horizon–scheme settings equally. A descriptive error ranking is distinct from a pairwise statistical decision and from a model–benchmark comparison.

METHOD / 06

Explanation construction

The principal channels are exact linear contributions, ARIMA native selected-lag counterfactuals, full two-seed ensemble TreeSHAP, and KernelSHAP summed into four lag groups for the sequence models. Native TFT selection and attention signals remain separate.

Fixed out-of-sample probe origins support consecutive-fold explanation comparisons. Static methods use four explained folds and 40 probes; neural methods use three explained folds and 30 probes. Explanation samples and forecast samples must not be conflated.

METHOD / 07

Temporal stability and regime coverage

Signed-vector Spearman correlation, cosine similarity, and greatest-absolute-feature agreement capture different aspects of explanation agreement. Positive vector rescaling can preserve ranks and cosine even when attribution magnitude changes.

Bull, bear, crisis, and boundary labels are ex post data-driven strata based on rolling summaries and full-series quantiles. They are not independently dated crises or deployable real-time signals. Sparse strata and adverse tails limit interpretation of high medians.

METHOD / 08

Faithfulness diagnostics

Additivity residuals test numerical reconstruction of the explained predictor. Deletion/insertion contrasts compare model-ranked interventions with equivalent random group orderings. PFI and LIME-style surrogates provide other diagnostics and can disagree with the main attribution channel.

The supplied exports contain intervention areas and success indicators, not all intermediate curve points. The public companion does not invent missing ALE or intervention curves. Additivity does not establish universal behavioral faithfulness.

METHOD / 09

Inference and multiplicity

The matched comparison uses observation-level squared-error differences and estimates autocovariances only within contiguous forecast blocks. The implemented long-run variance uses an unweighted sum through lag h minus one.

Each matched setting adjusts its 21 unordered model pairs using Benjamini–Hochberg. Native model–benchmark tests form separate 14-test primary families, with two-test sensitivity results retained. The reported study threshold is q = 0.05.

METHOD / 10

Bounded research findings

The financial means show no general black-box predictive advantage. All model mean relative RMSE values exceed persistence. LightGBM has the lowest mean relative RMSE, ARIMA the best mean rank, and Linear the strongest financial pairwise significance profile. No significant model win appears in the 224 native financial benchmark tests.

Explanation stability is conditional on model, diagnostic, and window. Numerical reconstruction is supported while broader intervention and cross-channel evidence is mixed. Lag-only observational inputs cannot identify genuine causal economic drivers. These statements do not prove that every observed association is spurious.

METHOD / 11

Reproducibility and public boundaries

The eight original executed notebooks, data, result CSVs, source-validation records, and figures are included in the public companion. Their original bytes remain available through source downloads and integrity hashes.

Replay uses recorded results; verified OLS sliders perform fixed-model inference. User-selected blends and teaching calculations are explicitly exploratory. Full training remains separate in Python/Jupyter. The complete thesis manuscript, Word/PDF files, and full-text transcripts are not published.

Historical scenarios

The four sliders select observed marginal values for lags 1, 5, 21 and 252. The reference origin supplies the initial profile. A custom combination can represent a profile that never occurred together. For each candidate date the app squares the four differences from the chosen inputs, averages them, then takes the square root. Smaller distances mean closer input profiles. Financial distances are displayed in percentage points of log return. Sunspot distances use native units.

Each dataset, horizon and window has 924 forecast origins shared by the seven models. The default search requires the candidate’s target date to fall strictly before the reference origin. The full archive also includes later outcomes. Cutoff ties are retained. Ties follow chronological order, with the reference first when eligible and exactly matched. Selecting a date shows that date’s recorded out-of-sample predictions. Displayed errors describe the date or selected subset.

Why identical inputs can lead to different results

In the rolling one-day Sunspot experiment, 152 forecast origins have zero at all four lag positions. Their observed next-day values range from 0 to 17. These dates can come from different fitted folds. ARIMA updates its state through the origin. The LSTM and simplified TFT also use 21 preceding feature rows. A matching four-lag profile therefore leaves parts of these models’ information sets unspecified.
